# Ad-Spend Decision Workbench: guided demonstration

CSV and synthetic campaign data. No advertising accounts are connected and no budget changes are executed.

## A focused walkthrough

1. **Reconcile inputs.**
2. **inspect margin.**
3. **compare scenarios.**
4. **review the narrative.**

Pause on the evidence at each decision: reconciliation before analysis; metric-bound narrative checks; explicit recommendation evidence.

## Boundaries

A controlled decision-support demo, not financial advice or a prediction of campaign returns. No automatic ad-platform writes exist in the showcased flow.
